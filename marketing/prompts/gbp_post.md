Write a short Google Business Profile post announcing a completed job or
update for AU Decorating.

What happened: {description}

Rules:
- Under 300 words, ideally much shorter (2-4 sentences).
- Naturally include the location if one is mentioned in "What happened" -
  don't invent a location if none was given.
- End with a soft call-to-action (e.g. inviting readers to get a free quote).
- No hashtags, no emoji, no marketing clichés ("Don't miss out!").
- Match the established brand voice: friendly, direct, not corporate.

Write only the post text, nothing else.
